How it works

✅

💬

Consent & opt-out

Daily Brief only messages people who have explicitly asked to receive it.

How users opt in: You subscribe by entering your own mobile number on this site and checking the box agreeing to receive messages. We record that consent. Phone numbers are never bought, rented, or shared, and no one is messaged who has not personally opted in. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency: Recurring — typically one briefing per day, plus replies to messages you send. Message and data rates may apply.

STOP

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You'll get one confirmation message and then no further texts.

HELP

Reply HELP for assistance, or email the operator at the address in the footer below.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Daily Brief ("we", "us") is an opt-in SMS news briefing service. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, which you provide in order to receive briefings.
  • A record of your consent (the date and that you agreed to receive messages).
  • Your chosen news topics and preferences.
  • The content of messages you send us, used only to generate a relevant reply.

How we use it

  • To send the daily news briefings you requested and to respond to your replies.
  • To remember and apply your topic preferences.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or message content with third parties.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.

Service providers

  • Messages are delivered through Twilio, our SMS provider. News content is summarized using a language model. These providers process data only to operate the service.

Data retention & opt-out

  • You can unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP. On request, your number and preferences are deleted from our records.

Questions about this policy? Contact the operator using the details in the footer.
