How it works

A simple, opt-in tool built for people who want the day's headlines without the noise.

✅

1. You opt in

You give explicit consent and pick your topics — for example AI, politics, business, or sports.

☀️

2. Morning briefing

Each morning we text you a handful of concise summaries with a link to read more on each story.

💬

3. Reply anytime

Text back to ask a question about a story or to change which topics you receive.

🛑

4. Stop anytime

Reply STOP to unsubscribe instantly. Reply HELP for assistance. No questions asked.

Consent & opt-out

Daily Brief only messages people who have explicitly asked to receive it.

How users opt in: A person subscribes by directly providing their own mobile number and confirming they want to receive a daily news briefing by SMS. Consent is collected in person or through a direct request to the operator. Phone numbers are never bought, rented, or shared, and no one is messaged who has not personally requested the service. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency: Recurring — typically one briefing per day, plus replies to messages you send. Message and data rates may apply.

STOP

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You'll get one confirmation message and then no further texts.

HELP

Reply HELP for assistance, or email the operator at the address in the Contact section below.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Daily Brief ("we", "us") is a personal, opt-in SMS news briefing project. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, which you provide in order to receive briefings.
  • Your chosen news topics and preferences.
  • The content of messages you send us, used only to generate a relevant reply.

How we use it

  • To send the daily news briefings you requested and to respond to your replies.
  • To remember and apply your topic preferences.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or message content with third parties.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.

Service providers

  • Messages are delivered through Twilio, our SMS provider. News content is summarized using a language model. These providers process data only to operate the service.

Data retention & opt-out

  • You can unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP. On request, your number and preferences are deleted from our records.
